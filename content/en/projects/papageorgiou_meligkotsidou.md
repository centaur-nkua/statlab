title: "Bayesian Allocation of Embedding Dimensions in Statistical Learning"
description: >-
  Selecting embedding dimensions for categorical predictors requires balancing representational
  fidelity, parameter cost, and uncertainty in the estimated embedding geometry. We develop a
  Bayesian allocation framework conditional on a fitted reference map, with a fixed-dimensional
  hierarchical prior on ordered singular values, allowing continuous spectral shrinkage and partial
  pooling across predictors. Predictor-specific posterior means and standard deviations of dis-
  carded spectral energy define an additive uncertainty-adjusted allocation criterion, minimized
  exactly under a shared embedding-parameter budget using dynamic programming.
